Raybox Bench – Help

Ray Box

  • Drag the ray box to move it; drag the rotation handle (circle) to rotate it.
  • Use the Power button to turn the beam on/off.
  • Use None / Single / Triple to switch slit attachments.

Placing Components

  • Click a component button in the toolbar to place it at the bench center.
  • Lenses: Convex Lens and Concave Lens, each in three variants (A, B, C). Which variant bends light most is part of the investigation — trace a beam through each one, or check the focal length in the properties panel.
  • Prisms: Prism-60 (equilateral, 60°–60°–60°) and Prism-90 (right-isosceles, 45°–45°–90°, right angle at the top).
  • Mirror: a flat plane mirror (100 mm) that reflects light from its front; its hatched back stops light.
  • Protractor: a 360° measurement overlay (1° ticks; prominent outer 0–90–0 scale, smaller inner 0–360° scale, labelled every 10°). It never touches the rays; grab its rim, center hub, or baseline to move it, and read its rotation (whole degrees) in the properties panel.
  • Drag the component body to move it.
  • Drag the rotation handle (circle above the component) to rotate it.
  • Click to select; press Delete key or the 🗑 Del button to remove.

Partial Reflection

On by default. Shows faint reflections at glass surfaces, becoming stronger near the critical angle. Uncheck to show only the main refracted ray and total internal reflection.

Properties Panel

  • Shows the variant name, position, rotation, focal length (lenses), or refractive index.
  • Lens focal length is editable — type a value and press Enter.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Z – Undo
  • Ctrl+Y – Redo
  • + / − – Zoom in / out · 0 – Reset zoom
  • Delete / Backspace – Delete selected
  • Escape – Deselect
